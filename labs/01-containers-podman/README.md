# Lab 01 — Containers (Podman)

**Objective:** containerize a small app with Podman — image layers, ports,
running a non-root container.

**Timebox:** ~45 min. If you're past 90, stop and ask in the course
discussion instead of pushing through alone.

**Where the work happens:** Red Hat Academy **DO188** — *OpenShift
Development I: Introduction to Containers with Podman* (v4.18), on
rha.ole.redhat.com. Your instructor gives you access; you complete the
guided lab there.

**Grading:** entirely from your RHA DO188 lab completion. Your instructor
assigns your grade from the RHA portal — there's nothing to submit in this
repo for this lab.

**FOSS equivalent:** everything in DO188 works identically with Docker if
you ever lose RHA access — `podman` and `podman-compose` are drop-in CLI
replacements for `docker` and `docker-compose`; the Containerfile syntax
you write is byte-for-byte a Dockerfile. Nothing you learn here is
Red-Hat-specific.
